Research

Carbon storage, microbial activity, root–microbiome interactions, and water movement are all shaped by soil structure: the arrangement of pores, particles, and interfaces.

How pore-scale architecture connects to system-level behavior remains one of the more challenging and unresolved questions in soil science. We bridge structural and biogeochemical approaches to understand soils as integrated systems, from individual pores to agricultural fields.
