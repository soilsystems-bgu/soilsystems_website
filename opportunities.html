Join us

We are looking for curious and motivated people to join the lab as MSc or PhD students or postdoctoral researchers, and to explore how soil structure shapes soil function, from pores to fields.

We welcome applicants from soil science, plant science, environmental science, engineering, and related fields. If our research interests you, get in touch: we are happy to discuss your own ideas as well as our ongoing projects.

What we look for

  • Background in science or engineering
  • Strong curiosity and motivation for research
  • Quantitative skills (statistics and data analysis)
  • Programming experience (Python, R, MATLAB, etc.)
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively

How to apply

Send your CV and a short note about your background and interests to maozd@bgu.ac.il.

Email us

Postdoctoral candidates are encouraged to reach out to discuss fellowship opportunities.
