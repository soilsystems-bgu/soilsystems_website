MSc / PhD · Open

Soil-on-a-chip: how pore networks govern carbon dynamics

How do soil pore networks regulate microbial activity and carbon cycling? This project combines microfluidics, X-ray tomography, and image analysis to study these processes in controlled, soil-like pore structures.

With Victor Yashunsky

MSc · Open

Microplastics in agricultural soils

A funded project uncovering how microplastics move, accumulate, and interact within agricultural soils, using X-ray microtomography (µCT) and deep learning-based image analysis.

Postdoctoral opportunities arise periodically. Candidates with expertise in soil science, stable isotope analysis, synchrotron techniques, or related fields are encouraged to reach out.

What we're looking for

  • Background in science or engineering
  • Strong curiosity and motivation for research
  • Strong quantitative skills (statistics and data analysis)
  • Programming experience (Python, R, MATLAB, etc.)
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively

How to apply

Send your CV and a short note about your background and interests to maozd@bgu.ac.il. Mention the project you are interested in, if any.
